usage: nzbpostarr --headless queue job [-h] {pause,resume,stop,retry,promote} ...

positional arguments:
  {pause,resume,stop,retry,promote}
                        Per-job commands
    pause               Pause a running job
    resume              Resume a paused job
    stop                Stop a job
    retry               Queue a new attempt from a failed job's saved request
    promote             Move a queued job to the front of the queue

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
